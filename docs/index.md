      <h2>Climate and body size</h2>
      <p>Our planet's climate is warming, and higher heat loads threaten the survival of ourselves and other species on the Earth. Having a smaller body size could make it easier to off-load excess body heat by reducing the distance that it needs to travel to reach the body's surface. We question this narrative and ask <i> "could climate change really be driving body sizes down?"</i>. </p>
      <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08131-7" class="learn-more-btn">Read More</a>

    <div class="image-block-text">
      <h2>Temperature and stress</h2>
      <p>Coping with stressors, like predators, social conflict or caring for offspring, consumes significant energy. So too does coping with cold-snaps and heat-waves. Yet animals, like us, commonly survive both types of challenges at once. How? We show that body temperature regulation is compromised under stress to balance energy loads; an effective short-term solution, but risky in a warmer world. </p>
      <a href="https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rspb/article/290/2010/20231251/104197" class="learn-more-btn">Read More</a>
